delivery\_issue: package delayed, lost, or tracking shows no movement



damaged\_or\_wrong\_item: customer received broken, wrong, or defective product



refund\_return: customer wants money back or wants to return item



account\_access: login, password, order history, account settings issues



billing\_payment: charged wrong amount, double charge, payment method issue



general\_complaint: frustration/anger with no specific actionable issue above



product\_inquiry: question about a product before/without a complaint



other: doesn't fit any category (spam, unclear, unrelated)
