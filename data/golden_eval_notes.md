Sampled 200 examples via random sampling from 8000 cleaned AmazonHelp pairs.
Labeled manually by reading each customer message and matching to one of 8 predefined intents.
Escalation flagged based on: angry tone, refund requests, legal/safety mentions, or issues
requiring account-specific verification a bot cannot perform.
Labeling took around 3 hours, done in one sitting to keep criteria consistent.
